LomoAmiga Mina Cho Snaps Lush Midsummer Portraits with the Half-Frame Simple Use Relodable Film Camera

In search of the lush green vitality of midsummer and a touch of nostalgic early-2000s freshness, LomoAmiga Mina Cho headed to Yeouido Saetgang Ecological Park in Seoul with the Half-frame Simple Use Reloadable Film Camera Classicolor. Combining built-in color gel flash filters, vertical framing, soap bubbles, and creative lighting, a fantastical summer scene was brought to life within the half-frame format. Set against a backdrop of retro music, check out her honest and charming story about this location shoot and the liberating perspective that analogue photography brings.
