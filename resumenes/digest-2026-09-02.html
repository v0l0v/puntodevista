Twelve Centuries of Documents Illuminate Humans’ Quest for Knowledge in ‘World Unfolding’

When a portrait of aviation pioneer Ruth Rowland Nichols was taken while she stood beside her Lockheed Vega aircraft in 1931, she was already one of the most accomplished pilots in the world. That photo was captured the year she also became the first woman to hold simultaneous international records in altitude, speed, and long distance. Nichols' striking portrait is just one of hundreds of images and illustrations highlighting humans' pursuit of knowledge in Worlds Unfolding: Science on the Page, published by Princeton University Press. The five-volume tome features more than 625 illustrations and an introduction by engineer and PBS personality Bill Nye. Moreover, it's an exploration of global science, medicine, and technology through the remarkable archives of The Huntington in San Marino, California.

Peter Seljan is living proof that even with a busy 9-5 job, you can still master street photography. After an eye surgery prompted him to pick up the camera, he never looked back.
