Now & Then in London: To Be (in Frame) or Not To Be

We’ve been to Vienna, Paris and Milan in our _Now & Then_ series, where we use archival film photos and give them a certain Lomographic flair. We now find ourselves in London, a city technically classified as a forest, one with the oldest subway in the world, and where over 300 languages are spoken. I took along a few photos as inspiration, my Lomomatic 110 and Color Tiger 110 ISO 200, to recreate history.
