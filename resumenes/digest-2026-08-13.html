‘Turn the Volume Up!’ Highlights the History of Speaking Out for Change in America

From the collection of the Nasher Museum of Art at Duke University, which features a wealth of artwork and artifacts related to social justice and community organizing, a new exhibition highlights American democracy and freedom of speech through the lens of art as an agent of change. Turn the volume up! includes works by a diverse group of contemporary artists and photographers, from Spider Martin’s dramatic documentation of Freedom Marches during the Civil Rights Movement to Kara Walker’s multimedia interrogations of U.S. history and racial stereotypes. Visitors will also encounter works by the Guerilla Girls, Carrie Mae Weems, Kiki Smith, Bruce Davidson, and many more.

The Ocean Photographer of the Year Finalists Highlight the Sorrowful and Sublime

Our oceans are places of mystery and biological wonders as much as they are essential to communities' livelihoods. We tap these expansive bodies of water for recreation, food, transportation. From the Atlantic to the Arctic, oceans cover around 70 percent of the earth's surface, and there is an incredible wealth of life yet to be discovered. As the continuing climate crisis melts ice caps and raises sea levels, and industrial fishing and waste threatens endangered marine animals and habitats, reminders of the oceans' irreplaceable value and power are ever more critical. That's where Oceanographic magazine and Blancpain’s annual Ocean Photographer of the Year contest comes in as a catalyst for conservation.

Tiny Questions with the Lomomatic 110: Aimee France

How much can we discover about someone through a handful of tiny questions? In this series, we’re exploring that very idea. With the tiny Lomomatic 110 in hand, we’re capturing the personalities, routines, and little moments that make someone who they are. Not long backstories, but honest answers, everyday rituals, and the unexpected details that reveal a life. This time, we sat down with Aimee France for a very Aimee edition of Tiny Questions. Whether it's a three-drink minimum at breakfast or baking cakes that look like they've come straight out of a storybook, Aimee is known for her love of food, baking, and finding beauty in the quirky details of the everyday.
