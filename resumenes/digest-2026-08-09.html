In Depth: A Grand Tour of Travel Photography

You remember that feeling of the last day before summer vacation, sitting in your last class of the day, the entire student body buzzing? When half of your peers hadn't bothered to show up, and the other half count down the minutes, no seconds, until the clock strikes summer – that moment you've been working towards all year? Now, let’s go back a few hundred years, to a world where apparently only men can get an education, everyone smelled a lot worse, and paintings were the only way to see the world around you. The grandiose Grand Tour was taken by young men (perhaps the sixth Alexander in their family) across Europe, as a way to experience the whole wide world (a.k.a. homogeneous European countries), and live the _dolce vita_ lifestyle.
