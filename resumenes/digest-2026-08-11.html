Joel Byron on Filming Reggae duo The Tribulizations in Brighton with the Joseph Petzval 55 f/1.7 Art Lens

UK-based director, photographer and videographer Joel Byron set out to capture a gig in his home town of Brighton by Reggae duo, The Tribulizations. He took along the Joseph Petzval 55 f/1.7 Focus-coupled Bokeh Control Art Lens, and let the looseness and personality of the lens capture the mood of the event. Joel talked to us about his experiences working with this unique bokeh lens and how letting go of your creative control can lead to some unexpected results.

When in Rome: The Corners of the Eternal City with Ugo Leonetti and Johannes Beilharz

What's it like to be a film shutterbug living in some of the most well-known, iconic cities in the world? Our new series explores these cities through the eyes of local Lomographers, who share emerging, classic, and underrated art, music, food and historical spots, for anyone looking to immerse themselves in the soul of the sprawling metropolis. We now have over 100 (and counting...) Travel Guides on our website, full of photography and travel inspiration tips around the globe. This time, we thought that we would let Lomographers themselves give us all the pieces we needed to create a Travel Guide for the romantically-ancient city of Rome.
