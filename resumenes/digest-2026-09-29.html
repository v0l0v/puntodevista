Author and Artist Leanne McPhee on Chrysotype, the Alternative Process of Photographic Printing in Gold

Our Magazine series, _Creators in Focus_, turns to creative minds from diverse disciplines whose work carries the spirit of analogue: tactile in process, rhythmic in pace, and shaped by time. As we learn more about alternative analogue processes, we discover the many unique ways artists have experimented with natural materials. Such was the case with author and artist Leanne McPhee, who, in 2020, published a comprehensive contemporary guide on the process of Chrysotype, or photographic printing using gold and iron. The process yields a wide range of hues, from pink, purple to blue, green and black.

LomoWalk Recap: Transforming a Sculpture Park into a Light Painting Lab with Lomo MC-A

You've probably gotten used to shooting in daylight, framing each shot through the viewfinder. Or, maybe you're a Lomographer who already knows the beauty of shooting from the hip; to “feel first, frame second”. We recently took it a step further and turned a sculpture park into a light painting lab, with the Lomo MC-A as a tool of choice. Photos by Ping Luo and Luxuan Chen with the Lomo MC-A On the evening of July 10, 10 participants gathered at Jing'an Sculpture Park, armed with the Lomo MC-A 35 mm Film Camera loaded with the LomoChrome Classicolor 35 mm ISO 200, along with light painting tools. They turned the park into a laboratory for light painting in the dark.
