Alba Petrichor’s Analogue Journal: The Beauty of the Everyday on Her Trip to Gran Canaria

For the Analogue Journal Magazine series, we invited photographer Alba Petrichor to take a moment of calm and capture her special moments with the Lomo’Instant Automat Glass camera and the Softcover Photo Album.
