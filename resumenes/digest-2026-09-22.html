In our era of digital screens and virtually endless images and videos, it's a challenge to imagine the time before photography existed. Two centuries ago this year, French inventor Nicéphore Niépce successfully captured the first known permanent image. Classical Musicians Interpret Astronauts’ Footage of the Planet for Earth Music Theater

On the International Space Station (ISS), a module known as the Cupola allows astronauts to float into a small, window-filled viewing area. It's used for observing high-tech exercises, spacewalks, and incoming vehicles, but it's also a spectacular place to view what's right outside: Earth. Astronauts often take thousands of photos and videos of what they see from their lofty vantage point, and the Association of Space Explorers (ASE)—the professional association of astronauts who have flown on a mission, representing 43 countries—archives the footage for educational use. So far, the collection consists of more than seven million photos and movies. What to do with all of them? Enter Earth Music Theater.
