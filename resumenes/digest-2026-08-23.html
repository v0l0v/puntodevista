Capturing the Invisible City: @filmlife_ww on Recording the Lingering Warmth of Hong Kong with the Lomo MC-A

We have invited @filmlife_ww to use the Lomo MC-A Film Camera as a creative tool. Paired with the Lomography Color Negative 35 mm ISO 400 and Lomography Color Negative 35 mm ISO 800, he expands his lens from everyday street photography to the preservation of local Hong Kong culture. By documenting vanishing collective memories like neon signs, he explores the delicate balance between “imagery and boundaries” in street photography. Through his visuals, he attempts to freeze the city's lingering warmth, inviting readers to notice and record the seemingly ordinary scenes that carry profound cultural weight.

Poet-Photographer Satellite June on the Intersection Between Poetry & Analogue Photography

As it turns out, poetry and photography share a deeply common language. Netherlands-based creator Nikki, also known as Satellite June, proves exactly that in her new book, _The Many Deaths of My Mother_. Armed with a typewriter and a collection of film cameras, she set out to confront her grief. Whether in black and white or color, medium or 35 mm format, the reader gets to see images turned deeply personal. In this article, she opens up about the cathartic effects of making art.
