Community Gallery: Finding Fresh Perspectives Through Reflections

In physics, it’s explained that when a wave of light hits a smooth surface (e.g., a mirror, glass, polished metal), it bounces right back and creates a visual illusion that the object is sitting behind the surface. We've always known that photography is closely tied to science, and this phenomenon is a great example of the ways the two can come together. In photography, reflections can be a pretty powerful creative tool. If you’re in search of a simple way to take cooler photos, look around you and find a reflective surface to create playful illusions and unique compositions that make us look twice. To give you examples, we’ve put together this gallery from the Community incorporating reflection into film photos.
