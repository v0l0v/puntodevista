Reimagining Films on Film: The Devil Wears Prada

Last year, the Lomography Magazine team came together for a special Writer’s Notes piece called Reimagining Films on Film – a reflective essay based on films that deeply resonate with us. I remember it as a lovely opportunity to reflect on our personal experiences during the holiday season while also celebrating the ways movies have influenced our perspectives. With that in mind, I wanted to pick up where we left off and continue with another film reimagined through analogue photography – another 2000s gem that shaped a lot of what I dreamt of back then and what I strive for now. Recently, I had the chance to watch the long-awaited sequel to The Devil Wears Prada. Seeing the story continue in the modern age prompted me to reflect on what the original meant to me growing up and how I resonate with what the sequel advocates for.
