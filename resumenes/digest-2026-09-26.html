Experimenting with the Invisible: Hugh Turvey on the Art of Xogram Photography

Hugh Turvey is a UK-based artist whose photography background led him into unique territory with the development of the _xogram_, an experimental technique using x-ray photography and black-and-white film. The xogram takes the historical techniques of photograms to a futuristic plane, bringing invisible imagery into the world of art and science. Hugh spoke to Lomography Magazine about some of his innovative installations and projects, from creating interactive smartwatch technologies to humanizing healthcare experiences through his discipline-defying work.
