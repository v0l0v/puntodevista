Diverse and Dramatic Behaviors Top the 2026 Bird Photographer of the Year Contest

It's all things avian at Bird Photographer of the Year, the world's largest contest dedicated to images of feathered creatures planet-wide. This year, the program welcomed more than 24,000 entries from photographers around the globe, capturing diving penguins, urban denizens, mating rituals, dramatic flights, and more. Highlighting birds of all kinds, the competition emphasizes the diverse appearances and behaviors of the animals and also raises awareness of the challenges they face due human-triggered habitat loss, poaching, pollution, and other factors. Categories include Best Portrait, Bird Behavior, Conservation, Urban Birds, and others, plus three youth categories for budding photographers aged 17 years and younger.

In the Frame: Unconventional Portraits

We often assume that portraits, by default, show us a person's characteristics, and that the eyes are the window to the soul. While in most cases it's true, we also speak through body language – how some of us cover our faces when laughing, how we lean unconsciously towards someone telling us a story, how we express certain emotions with the tilt of our heads. In this edition of In the Frame, we're calling on our Community to shoot and share unconventional portraits taken with that slightly skewed angle, candid expressions and all!
