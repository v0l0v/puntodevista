Three Photographers, One Model: Different Ways of Seeing With the Lomo MC-A

What happens when three photographers are given the same camera, the same model, and the same location? The shooting setup may be identical, but surely, the way every photographer sees through their lens is different, right? The Lomography Vienna team explored that idea as Elisa Parrino, Barbara Podstawczynska, and Tony Sotelo went out with their Lomo MC-A cameras in hand to take portraits of model Ginevra Mariottini. Each photographer loaded with a different film stock to interpret the scene: LomoChome Purple, LomoChrome Classicolor, and LomoChrome Sun-kissed '92. Read along to see how each photographer brought their unique visions to life!

A Day With Mineho Osawa: In Tune with Music, At Peace with Myself

Growing up surrounded by music, Mineho Osawa, drummer of trio band amenoparade, spends her every day fully immersed in music. Away from the stage, however, life slows down. At home, she shares her days with two talkative cats she's lived with for the past six years, and finds moments of calm among the plants she loves – small rituals that gently reset her mind. For this feature of A Day With, we invited Mineho to capture these quiet yet meaningful moments of her everyday life using the Lomo’Instant Automat Glass. Join us for a glimpse into her day, preserved through palm-sized instant photographs.
