Chromogenic print, 36 x 45 inches Highlighting more than 250 women in the field of photography, Great Women Photographers is organized alphabetically by name. From photojournalism and editorial portraiture to cameraless images and conceptual landscapes, Great Women Photographers is not only a visual record of the diverse contributions made by women but also, as the publisher notes, a new lens on the world, inviting readers to discover, engage with, and be inspired by the work of some of the world’s greatest image-makers. You may also enjoy exploring other titles in the series, including Great Women Artists , Great Women Painters , and Great Women Sculptors .
