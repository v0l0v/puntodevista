The Camera That Brought Me Home: Marc Haeffner Tests the Lomomatic 110

Earlier this year, Marc Haeffner took our Berlin Kino B&W 35 mm ISO 400 Film back to its namesake and spoke about his experience in an inspiring interview. A few weeks later, he came back with an exciting pitch for a summer series on pocket film, and we were more than happy to be involved in it! We sent him a Lomomatic 110 Camera and some of our 110 films so he could experiment with this format. True to his creative spirit, Marc decided to split his introduction to pocket photography into two parts, exploring two completely different concepts on the same camera (and keeping us on the edge of our seats for the results). In this first feature, Marc shares the motivation behind his project and what continues to fascinate him about 110 film. He takes us along on his initial shoot and offers a glimpse into the experiences that gave this article its title.
