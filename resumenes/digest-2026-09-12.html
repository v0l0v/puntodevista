The Places We Call Home: Ala Kheir on the Sudan Family Archives

Home is as much a feeling as a place, and one photograph can bring back memories that may often feel far away. Working with collections of family photography from his home country of Sudan, photographer Ala Kheir began the _Sudan Family Archives_ project, which seeks to protect and preserve home, heritage, and humanity for present and future generations. Family photography archives have been key to connecting with Sudan's visual culture and history, as well as forging new relationships and legacies in uncertain times. Ala spoke to Lomography Magazine about the value of these everyday visuals and how family knowledge lays the foundation for collective memory.
