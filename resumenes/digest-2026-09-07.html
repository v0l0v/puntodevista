From the Archives of The Family Museum: Historical Tintypes Collection

Tintype photography was once a popular medium for capturing formal and casual portraits. Across America, and later the United Kingdom, travelling and studio photographers immortalized countless faces of ordinary people. This repository, much of it hidden from the public eye, waits for archivists and collectors avidly browsing auctions, flea market stalls, and second-hand shops. In this interview, Nigel Shephard of London-based archival project The Family Museum (@the-family-museum) speaks about the history behind the tintypes recently shared on Lomography.
